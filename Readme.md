## 💡 Strategic Insights & Key Findings

- **Fintech & Software Moat**: Fintech and SaaS maintain the highest capital concentration due to scalable ARR multiples.
- **Decacorn Giants**: ByteDance, SpaceX, and Stripe hold aggregate valuations exceeding $400 Billion combined.
- **AI Acceleration**: Generative AI startups achieved unicorn valuations in under 3.2 years, faster than any prior technology cycle.
- **Global Distribution**: India emerged as the #3 startup ecosystem globally with over 100 verified unicorns.
